# Halo Weave
**Weave. Dodge. Overdrive.**

## About Halo Weave
Halo Weave is a fast, neon arcade game where you guide a glowing orb around a ring of barriers. Tap to weave between lanes, dodge danger, grab power-ups and build huge combos. Easy to learn, hard to put down. Short runs, big thrills, and no account needed.

## Key Features

* **Five Game Modes:** Classic, Time Attack, Flip Arena, Zen Flow and a Daily Challenge with a new twist every day.
* **Overdrive and Combos:** Chain close calls, build your multiplier and trigger Overdrive for a burst of power.
* **Power-Ups:** Shields, slow-motion, magnets, blasts and more to change every run.
* **Collectible Orbs:** Unlock orbs from Common to Mythic. Higher rarity means bigger animations and special perks.
* **Missions and Journey:** Daily missions and long-term goals that reward coins and gems.
* **Upgrades:** Spend coins on permanent upgrades and make your orb stronger.
* **Fair and Friendly:** Optional reward ads only for extra rewards, and a one-time Halo Pack to remove ads.
* **Privacy-First:** No sign-up, no login. Your progress stays on your device.

## Tech Stack

* **Framework:** Flutter (Dart) with a custom game engine and canvas rendering.
* **Monetization:** Google AdMob (ads) and Google Play Billing (one-time purchase).
* **Storage:** On-device local storage. No servers.
